\vskip 2em

We organize our exploration around two broad questions.

First, we characterize the \textit{determinants} of multinational presence in trade, examining how the share of MNEs in exports and in the number of exporters varies across exporting countries, export destinations, and products. We relate these patterns to country characteristics (income, distance, trade agreements), product characteristics (upstreamness, complexity, quality, elasticity of substitution), and bilateral trade costs.

Second, we study the \textit{effects} of multinational presence on trade patterns. Here, we ask whether trade routes and products with greater MNE participation exhibit larger trade flows, different responses to gravity, distinct concentration structures, and broader extensive margins. We further exploit the full corporate network structure available in ORBIS and DnB to test whether MNEs trade differently with destinations where their parent or affiliates are located. Throughout, we document these patterns both in aggregate and by exporting country, allowing the reader to assess which findings are common across the region and which are country-specific.

\subsection{MNE Network Regressions}
\label{subsec:network}
%------------------------------------------------------------------

\begin{itemize}
    \item[\ding{114}] \textit{Sample:} firm-destination-year. \textit{Three decompositions} (D1--D3) of how MNE corporate network structure at the destination shapes the sensitivity of trade to distance, across the standard 6-spec FE ladder. D1 tests whether MNEs trade differently with destinations where their corporate network is present vs.\ absent. D2 further separates HQ vs.\ affiliate presence. D3 adds a neighbor indicator. MNE\_ext and MNE\_total produce qualitatively similar patterns; MNE\_total results shown first as the main specification, followed by MNE\_ext.
    \begin{itemize}
        \item D1: MNE$\times$Present$\times$Dist,\ MNE$\times$NotPresent$\times$Dist,\ DOM$\times$Dist
        \item D2: MNE$\times$HQ$\times$Dist,\ MNE$\times$Aff$\times$Dist,\ MNE$\times$NotPr$\times$Dist,\ DOM$\times$Dist
        \item D3: MNE$\times$HQ$\times$Dist,\ MNE$\times$Aff$\times$Dist,\ MNE$\times$Neighbor$\times$Dist,\ MNE$\times$NoPres$\times$Dist,\ DOM$\times$Dist
    \end{itemize}
\end{itemize}
