## 2. Sample and definitions

- Nine origins (ARG, CHL, COL, CRI, DOM, PER, PRY, SLV, URY; ECU excluded), 2012 to 2018, HS 2017 codes, real
  destinations only, agricultural products only (860 products; 782 in 2018).
- **MNE**: exporter matched to Orbis or D&B. **Local firm**: not matched. Foreign / domestic MNE by parent country.
- **Capital intensity (KL_p)**: capital per worker of the US farm type (Census of Agriculture 2017, farm stage) or
  industry (NBER-CES, processing stage) the product comes from. 22 farm groups and 26 processing groups (`src_id`);
  every product of a group shares its value.
- **Cells of the event studies**: A = product cells (origin x product), B = destination cells (origin x destination x
  product), E = new destination cells (B cells where local firms did not export before the MNE entered the product).
  Event = first year with an MNE exporting in the cell.
- **Comparison rules (treatment definitions)** in `wp_agro_v5_events.py`:

| Code | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `g_ps` | **Main.** Treated need two years with exports before t0; each never treated cell draws a pseudo t0 from the cohort distribution (seed 20261124) and must also have two years with exports before it. |
| `g_v4` | Rule of the v4 draft: treated need two years before t0, never treated only two years with exports at any time (asymmetric). |
| `g_nc` | No condition. `g_bal` cells exporting every year. `g_pre3` three years before t0 (pseudo t0 for controls). |
| `g_for`, `g_dom`, `g_s10`, `g_s25`, `g_pers` | First foreign MNE, first domestic MNE, MNE with >= 10% / >= 25% of the cell's exports at t0, MNE still present at t0 + 1. Controls of the v4 rule. |
| `g_forps` ... `g_persps` | Same events with the controls of `g_ps` (built inside the do-file, block `treatps`). |
| `g_be` | Cells E. |

- Counts (treated / controls): A g_v4 405/746, A g_ps 405/509; B g_v4 3,302/13,648, B g_ps 3,302/8,747; E 1,289/438.
- **Estimators**: TWFE (reghdfe), Callaway and Sant'Anna (csdid 2.0.3: `cs` not yet treated, `csnev` never treated with
  option `never`), Sun and Abraham (eventstudyinteract), Borusyak, Jaravel and Spiess (did_imputation), de Chaisemartin
  and D'Haultfoeuille (did_multiplegt_dyn), stacked by cohort (by hand), Wooldridge PPML (jwdid), honestdid,
  Freyaldenhoven, Hansen and Shapiro (xtevent), CEM matched stacked (Python).
- **Event FE codes**: EV1 cell + year; EV2 cell + origin x year (B: + destination x year); EV3 A: + type x year, B: cell +
  ODY; EV4 A: + HS2 x year, B: + product x year; EV5 B: cell + OPY; EV2T imputation with cell trends; EV2TF cell trends
  over the full window. Clusters: cell for A, origin and destination pair for B and E.
- **Regression FE codes** (unchanged from v4): FE3 ODP + Y, FE4 ODP + ODY, FE5 + OPY, FE6 + DPY. Location regressions:
  origin x year + type of production (main), origin x year + HS chapter, origin x year alone.

### 4.2 Capital intensity and the exports-MNE association (sheets 2.x; spec E9)

- **E9a ladder** (lnN, ln exports of all firms, FE4). Farm: high rises from 0.84 (cut-off US$30k) to about 1.14 once
  the cut-off isolates field crops (stage ratio 1.135 vs low 0.669; export weighted median 1.139 vs 0.675; US$150k 1.159
  vs 0.681); low stays at about 0.67 throughout; at US$261k high has one product. Processing: high vs low never differs
  robustly (stage ratio 0.751 vs 0.865, i.e. reversed; median 0.895 vs 0.766; US$240k 0.836 vs 0.786). Same pattern
  with FE6 (farm high about 1.0 to 1.18, low about 0.5).
- **E9b by farm type or industry** (the flexible specification). Farm: one coefficient stands out, grains and oilseeds
  (US$261k per worker, 48.6% of farm exports): 1.16 at FE4 and 1.17 at FE6, against 0.5 to 0.74 for the other large
  groups. Slope of the group coefficients on ln KL: farm FE4 0.19 (SE 0.03) export weighted, 0.12 (SE 0.06) unweighted;
  FE6 0.27 (0.08) and 0.02 (0.13). Processing FE4 -0.03 (0.07) and -0.03 (0.11); FE6 -0.00 (0.17) and -0.18 (0.21). All
  groups equal rejected in every case. **Reading: there is no capital intensity gradient; the farm "high KL" premium is
  the grain and oilseed group.** (Figure 6.)
- **E9c** one cut-off for both stages: results in sheet 2.4 (mixes the farm premium with the flat processing pattern).
- **E9d other US measures** (farm, lnN, FE4): farm assets per worker gives the same split as KL_p (1.139 vs 0.675);
  machinery per dollar of sales 1.062 vs 0.683; **machinery per acre reverses the sign (0.730 vs 0.838, p = 0.078)**.
  Processing: capital stock per worker identical to KL_p (0.895 vs 0.766); capital per value added 0.892 vs 0.761.
- **E9e country check**: rank correlation between the origin's export weighted lnKL and FAOSTAT capital per agricultural

## 5. Dead results (do not use)

- ~~Survival effect of v4: probability of local exports -15 points (A) / -18 points (B) after entry~~. Artifact of the
  asymmetric control rule (g_v4). With the same rule (g_ps) or matched controls it is about zero. Never compare event
  results across treatment definitions without naming the definition.
- ~~Large entry -0.25 / -0.28~~ (v4 controls). Halved with the same rule: -0.12 / -0.14.
- ~~v4 "liberal Rauch"~~: those `lib_*` columns were Nunn input weights, not Rauch. Replaced by the complete Rauch file.
- ~~E9b inverse variance weighted slope~~: dominated by a small poultry group with SE 0.003; replaced by export weighted
  and unweighted slopes.

## 7. Traps found in this round (also in CLAUDE.md)

- **Never edit a do-file while a Stata batch run is reading it**: Stata reads line by line; four B blocks died with parse
  errors after up to two hours. Each run now executes its own copy (`ev.do`) in its own folder.
- csdid 2.0.3 uses not yet treated by default; never treated needs the option `never` (the first std run repeated cs).
- did_multiplegt_dyn `trends_nonparam()` needs a numeric variable; jwdid needs hdfe; honestdid `l_vec` must be a column
  matrix; xtevent needed window(-4 3).
- The ladder labels must be keyed by stage (`Stage|col`) or the second stage overwrites the first.
- Python `-I` has no pandas on this machine: run scripts from the repo with the normal interpreter.

## 8. The story as it stands (for the text, not yet written)

1. MNEs are not more present in capital intensive products (farm: less present; processing: flat).
2. The exports-MNE association does not grow with capital intensity; the farm premium is grains and oilseeds.
3. The association is weaker in complex and differentiated products.
4. After entry, the probability that local firms export does not change (v4's fall was the control group).
5. Local firms turn over more: new local exporters up (clean pre-trends), exits up, local share down.
6. Only large entries (MNE >= 25% of the cell) displace local exporters: -14 points, clean pre-trends in A.
7. Destination cells point the same way but fail pre-trend tests, except balanced cells (local exports +0.21).
8. Effects on local firms are not larger in labor intensive products (H2 not supported).
