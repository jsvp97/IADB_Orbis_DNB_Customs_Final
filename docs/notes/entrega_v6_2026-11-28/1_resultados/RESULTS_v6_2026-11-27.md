# Agro paper, round v6 of estimates: what was done and what came out (2026-11-27)

Read this before touching the agro paper again. Complete record of round v6 (`especificaciones_estimaciones_v6_2026-11-25.md`,
repo root): request, rules, scripts, every result of the core including nulls and wrong signs, what could not be estimated and
why, traps, and the suggestions kept apart. Numbers are those of `docs/notes/WP_paperagro_v6_master_estimates.xlsx`; the
workbook wins. Round v5 is in `RESULTS_v5_2026-11-25.md`.

## 1. Request and rules

Comments on v5 (2026-11-27): (i) the pseudo-year rule of v5 was not aligned by cohort (each control compared with every cohort
although its prior-exports condition was evaluated at another year); redo with cohort-aligned stacks. (ii) The rise of new local
exporters was concentrated in cohorts 2014 and 2017; rule out that the 2017 part comes from the HS 2017 code change. (iii) In
the rows by cohort and placebo, the OLS average divided by five with fewer coefficients. Then: run the v6 document as written
(core first; second tier only after the core is delivered), propose anything missing separately. A primary set of six tests and
one diagnostic was fixed before running (Part C); everything else carries a Role. Text still frozen at v4. Logs only of positive
values; never ln(1 + x) or asinh.

**Status: the core (V6-01 to V6-18) is delivered. The second tier (V6-19 to V6-31) was not run.**

## 3. Definition answers (sheet 10.1), the ones that change the reading of v5

- g_ps counted any two years with exports of all firms before t0; cohort 2013 and cells with an MNE in 2012 were excluded.
- The pseudo-year only filtered: every control was compared with every cohort (237 of 746 product-cell controls lost to the draw;
  only seed 20261124 used).
- csdid in v5 had no fixed effects or covariates: it did NOT control for origin x year; its pre-period coefficients are
  period-to-period differences. SA, BJS and dCDH did control for origin-year.
- The stacked estimator of v5 had stack-specific FE and only never-treated controls.
- NEWL: first year in the cell, looking back to 2012; in destination cells "new" means new in the destination.
- Exporter class is fixed over time (no acquisition date). The whole customs data (all chapters) can be used for "new".
- Activity codes: NAICS and US SIC from Orbis, D&B primary NAICS; the WP extract keeps only Orbis affiliate NAICS-2, which covers
  32.6 percent of MNE export value.
- Quantities: kilograms exist in the raw customs file for all ten origins (97-100 percent of rows), not carried into the base.
- PCI6: the Atlas publishes PCI only at four digits; the OEC publishes six-digit PCI in HS 2017 (2018-2024) and HS 2012
  (2012-2024). Main column PCI6_hs17 as in the rule of V6-03.
- Freyaldenhoven et al. did not run in ANY v5 cell set (xtevent error 198); the v5 note saying it ran on product cells was wrong.

## 8. Caveats found while running (to be read with the results)

1. **anyXL is positive by construction in part.** Entered cells export in g (the MNE exports), comparison cells need not; under
   R(g) local exports are positive in g - 1 and g - 2 in both groups, so a cell that stops exporting altogether is always a
   comparison cell. T6 passes, but the +0.12 measures in part the survival of the cell, not of local firms.
2. **The 2017 cohort and HS 2017**: half of its k = 0 new exporters are in hs_n1 codes; without the 2017 cohort T5 falls to
   +0.19 (n.s.), without hs_n1 codes it rises to +0.30. The code change does not create the effect, but the 2017 cohort carries
   part of its precision.
3. PCI6_hs17 is measured in 2018-2024 (the only years of the HS 2017 series), after the sample.
4. Panel F of V6-05 cannot separate ln XM from ln (XM / N) (identity).

## 10. Traps of this round (also in CLAUDE.md)

- In Stata, `foreach x in $CL hs4n` splits a two-way cluster list "od op" into two one-way clusters: quote it.
- `if _rc == 0 & `s' > 0` is evaluated even when `s' is empty and stops the do-file: test _rc first.
- Event dummies whose outcome is constant (anyXL two years before entry, N and D before entry) make the cluster covariance
  singular: reghdfe returns no standard errors at all. Drop those terms.
- `egen ..., label` fails on the second cut if the label exists: drop it first.
- The OEC tesseract API returns six-digit PCI with ids section*10^6 + HS6.
